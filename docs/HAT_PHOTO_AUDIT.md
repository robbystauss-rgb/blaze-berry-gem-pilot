235 active front photos; 8 historical 112PM entries excluded from public sales and new renditions. Full exact-photo IDs, decoded pixels and provenance are in [HAT_PHOTO_COVERAGE.csv](HAT_PHOTO_COVERAGE.csv). Low-detail sources must be replaced by owner-verified, higher-resolution photographs of the same model/colorway.

The front renderer uses canonical nominal inches, per-photo crown geometry and a retained, immutable source coordinate system. Without a measured reference, it uses the explicit 7.5-inch crown estimate; visual bounds are not physical measurements. Side/rear reference photos have no calibrated patch overlay. The obsolete percentage sizes no longer render current hat previews. Historical snapshots and coordinates remain stored unchanged.

Responsive derivatives resize the retained exact full-resolution photograph without upscaling or changing its crop. Selection follows painted CSS width × device density × zoom. Original identity/hash/calibration stays authoritative, and missing/wrong-size renditions fall back to the identical canonical photo. Low-detail sources disable extra zoom. No new photos, materials, views or services are manufactured.

Synthetic measured-reference crop fixtures test the mathematics only. They are not measurements of actual hats. Actual physical accuracy remains unverified until the owner supplies known reference lengths for each published photo.
