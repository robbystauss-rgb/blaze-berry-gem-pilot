## Specific open items for counsel

### Business identity

Confirm the person/entity legally operating REC Mama Made, any DBA/trade-name relationship, the principal business address, and the correct public legal-notice contact. Do not infer these from a payment handle, domain registration, account name, or user location.

### Customer-supplied content

Review the published ownership/authorization/limited-license language and decide whether to add a customer-content indemnification provision. A draft concept is kept in the internal legal/compliance review and is not published.

### Proofs

Confirm the distinction between customer-approved content errors and REC Mama Made production errors. Define the evidence required to show the exact final approved proof, proof version, and UTC approval timestamp.

### Refunds, remakes, cancellations

Approve any specific deadlines, cancellation stages, fees, remake charges, nonrefundable deposits, or defect/damage reporting windows before those rules are published or enforced.

### Shipping

Review actual production-time claims and the delayed-order process for compliance with the FTC Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule. Ensure operational communications distinguish production time from carrier transit time and do not use an overbroad “not responsible after carrier pickup” disclaimer.

### Privacy and retention

Confirm actual production analytics/cookie settings, the final list of service providers, retention periods, and any state-law privacy obligations that apply to the business. Current policy intentionally makes no GDPR/HIPAA/PCI-certification or fixed-retention claim.

### DMCA / Section 512

Determine whether REC Mama Made performs an activity for which a Section 512 safe harbor is relevant. If so, review repeat-infringer implementation, notice/counter-notice procedure, public designated-agent contact details, Copyright Office agent registration/renewal, and standard technical measures. The public site does not claim safe-harbor status.

### Liability, warranty, governing law, disputes

No limitation-of-liability cap, “AS IS” warranty disclaimer, Louisiana governing-law clause, venue clause, arbitration clause, jury waiver, or class-action waiver was published. Review and add only if appropriate after the legal operator and principal place of business are verified.
